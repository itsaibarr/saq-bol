# Hackathon context (source of truth for all agents)

Event: Veritas AI International High School Hackathon 2026 (highschoolhackathon.org). Full brief text: research/00-hackathon-briefs.txt
Window: Sat Nov 14 00:00 ET -> Sun Nov 15 23:59 ET (= Mon Nov 16 09:59 UTC+5). Firm deadline, no late entries.
Tracks: AI+Health, AI+Finance, AI+Climate, AI+Languages. Each allows "your own idea". One track per project.
Rules: AI/ML must do the main job; public or synthetic data only; cite every dataset/model/tool/AI assistant; report real results incl. weak ones; results on unseen test data; health/finance need "prototype, not medical/financial advice" line.
Every project must show: named user + moment of use; real input -> output; beats a simple baseline (report both); at least one failure example with explanation.
Track-specific judge focus:
- Health: clear user, false negatives and false positives reported separately, subgroup fairness (age, sex, skin tone).
- Finance: right metric, fair baseline, trust, fairness across groups even without direct attributes; time-based split.
- Climate: one place, persistence baseline, time-based split, decision link, small real place beats "the planet".
- Languages: side-by-side outputs, per-language/accent breakdown, real speaker check, lower-resource languages favored.
Round 1 (30% of final): Technical execution 30, Originality 20, Impact+track fit 20, Testing+honesty 15, Communication 15.
Finals (70%): live 5-min demo + Q&A: Demo/clarity 30, Technical depth 30, Impact 20, Q&A 20.
Judges: Kareema Batool (Microsoft; Harvard), Niranjani Prasad (Microsoft Research Cambridge, ML for healthcare), Abdulla Kerimov (Stanford PhD computational rock physics), more TBA.
Prizes: Gold = internship at Arecibo AI (healthcare AI startup, co-founded by Dr. Yanshan Wang, Univ. of Pittsburgh); also Best AI for Good, Judges' Choice, Best Beginner Build.
Deliverables: public Colab notebook or GitHub repo (outputs visible); ~1 page write-up (template in briefs); optional <=2 min demo video; credits list.
Compute available: free Kaggle GPU (CLI token present) + Google Colab + local Apple M5 16GB.
Team member is based in UTC+5 (very likely Kazakhstan; confirm before relying on it).
Time budget: ~43 h from Sat Nov 14 09:50 UTC. Research must finish fast; the build needs >= 30 h.
